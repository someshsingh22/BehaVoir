\caption{Overview of the benchmark. Given a research question taken from a published paper and a budget, the
\emph{Dataset Discovery Agent} works in a disposable workspace: guided by our data catalog, it explores a read-only
corpus snapshot (Reddit or GitHub) with SQL through our tools (or a plain shell) and saves a labelled dataset with
its methodology before the time limit. The generated and the authors' datasets are scored by downstream utility under
the same fixed training budget: a submission agent formats each dataset to the guidelines, then the authors' released
code (\emph{Author Evaluation Harness}) or our standardized pipeline (\emph{Open Evaluation Harness}) trains and
evaluates on it.}
\label{fig:approach}
